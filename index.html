How to Play Shithead

Goal: Clear all your cards (Hand, Face-Up, and Face-Down) as fast as possible. The last player left with cards is crowned the SHITHEAD!

Swap Phase: At the start, swap cards between your Hand and your 3 Face-Up table cards to set up your final line of defense!

Playing Cards: Must play a card equal to or HIGHER than the top card of the Play Pile. You can play multiple cards of the same rank together.

Special Power Cards:

  • 2 (Reset): Can be played on anything. Resets pile value to 2. (Optional Rule: Playing a 2 allows you to play again!)
  • 4 (Reverse - Optional): Can be played at ANY time on ANY card! Reverses the order of play (↻ Clockwise / ↺ Counter-Clockwise)!
  • 10 (Burn): Can be played on anything. Immediately burns the pile and gives you another turn!
  • 7 (Must Play ≤ 7): Next player must play a 7 or lower (or a wildcard 2, 10, 8, or 4).
  • 8 (Invisible / Transparent): Can be played on ANY card! It is invisible to the next player—they must play against whatever card is underneath the 8.
  • 4-of-a-Kind Burn: 4 identical cards in a row burn the pile!

How to Play & Drag & Drop:

  • Drag & Drop: Drag cards from your hand directly onto the Play Pile to play them! During the Swap Phase, drag a hand card onto any face-up table card to swap.
  • Tap / Click: Tap cards to select multiple matching cards of the same rank, then tap "Play Cards" or "Pickup Pile".

Picking Up: If you cannot or choose not to play a valid card, you MUST pick up the entire pile into your hand.
